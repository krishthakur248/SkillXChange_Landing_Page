Take control of cognitive health

Our AI-powered platform combines rapid cognitive testing, health questionnaires, and natural language analysis to detect both objective and subjective markers of cognitive change.

Impacts of cognitive decline and Alzheimer's disease are usually detected well into progression, when treatment options are limited.

Current diagnostic approaches rely on specialist reviews and clinical imaging that are often costly, slow, and inaccessible to many.

Mild early symptoms are frequently missed, creating a critical lag between initial cognitive changes and formal clinical diagnosis.

Many people live unaware, missing vital opportunities for early lifestyle interventions that could make a meaningful difference.

Neurofied.ai bridges this gap by bringing convenient, rapid, and scalable digital cognitive assessments directly to users everywhere.

By detecting subtle behavioral and expressive shifts early, we empower individuals to proactively manage brain health without clinical delay.

Three unified pathways

Pathway 1: Interactive behavioral testing measuring reaction speed, attention, and short-term memory metrics.

Pathway 2: Structured input evaluating age, lifestyle, and key cognitive markers using explainable risk models.

Pathway 3: Natural language processing for free-text symptom narratives and subjective user reports.
